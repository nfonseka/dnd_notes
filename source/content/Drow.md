- Dark Elf

- Drow form their cities in the [[Underdark]], moved there a millenia ago
- Been there long enough that they have adapted to it but they are sensitive to sunlight and will burn
	- Daylight spell will give them disadvantage on checks and makes them blind
	- The feydark and underdark drows were related but have formed seperated societies
- Most [[Underdark]] drow are worshippers of [[Lolth]]
	- There are some reformed drow called [[Seldarine]]
- Society is very matriarchal
	- Kept around just to reproduce
